Nur Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

Lantern Studios LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates Nur. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information stored on your device

Nur stores information on your device to personalize your experience, including your name, onboarding answers, preferences, bookmarks, reading progress, prayer records, goals, and selected location. Downloaded content may also be stored for offline use.

Nur currently does not require an account. We do not upload your name, bookmarks, reading progress, or prayer records to a Lantern Studios account or synchronization service. Selected onboarding answers, goals, and reminder settings are also collected for analytics as described below.

Location and permissions

With your permission, Nur uses your device’s location to calculate prayer times and the direction of the Qibla. Your selected coordinates and location label are stored on your device. Apple’s location and geocoding services may process coordinates to determine your location and place name.

Nur does not request background location access. Compass readings are used to provide Qibla guidance.

If you enable notifications, Nur schedules reminders locally on your device. You can change location and notification permissions at any time in your device’s Settings.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes payments for subscriptions purchased through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card or bank account details.

We use RevenueCat to validate purchases, manage subscription access, restore purchases, and understand subscription activity. RevenueCat processes purchase and subscription information, an automatically generated app user identifier, and related app and device information needed to provide its services.

These services are also governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy and RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

Analytics

We collect limited analytics to understand how people begin using Nur and improve the experience. This includes:

  • Onboarding screens reached, buttons tapped, completion, and time spent.
  • Confirmed onboarding answers about reasons for using Nur, faith routines, and personal reflections.
  • Selected reading goals, prayer reminders, and notification permission status.
  • Paywall activity, purchase and restore outcomes, and trial and subscription starts.
  • App version, build number, and event times.

Analytics records use a randomly generated installation ID. It is not your name, email address, or Apple ID. It helps us distinguish app installations and understand their activity. Reinstalling Nur or using another device may create a different ID.

We store analytics using Supabase. We also share the installation ID with RevenueCat to connect subscription events with app activity. Our reports show counts, while the underlying records retain installation IDs.

Your name, email address, precise location, bookmarks, reading history, and prayer records are not included in these analytics events.

Online content and technical information

Nur connects to hosting and content-delivery services, including Supabase, to retrieve published readings, images, and audio. When these connections occur, service providers may process technical information such as your IP address, requested resource, and request time to deliver content, maintain reliability, and protect their services.

If you contact us, we receive your email address and any information you include. We use this information to respond and provide support.

How we use and share information

We use information to operate Nur, provide its features, understand and improve the app experience, manage subscriptions, respond to support requests, maintain security, and meet legal obligations.

Information may be processed by service providers that support these functions. We may also disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, our services, or legal rights.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Nur does not include third-party advertising.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Information stored locally generally remains until it is changed, removed, or the app is deleted. Device backups may retain copies according to your Apple settings.

We retain support correspondence and service-related records only as reasonably necessary for their purposes, security, and applicable legal requirements. Apple and other service providers may retain records under their own policies and legal obligations.

Detailed analytics records currently have no automatic expiry. Deleting the app does not automatically delete analytics already received by our services.

Deleting Nur does not cancel an App Store subscription or delete purchase records held by Apple or RevenueCat. You can manage or cancel your subscription through your Apple account settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information we hold, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us to make a request. We may need to verify your identity, and applicable legal exceptions may apply. For analytics requests, we may also need information that helps us locate the relevant installation records.

Security and international processing

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own, where privacy laws may differ. Where required, appropriate safeguards apply to these transfers.

Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through our online services. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

External links

Nur may link to external websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Nur develops. We will post the updated policy on this page, revise its effective date, and provide additional notice where required.
